Residential — Kitchen Set

Oakwood
Minimalist

Location

Kemang, South Jakarta

Materials

Solid White Oak, Brass Inlays, Carrara Marble

Completion

October 2023

A blend of Scandinavian simplicity and bespoke Indonesian craftsmanship.

This project was designed for a family that values both social interaction and culinary precision. The challenge was to integrate a high-performance chef's kitchen into an open-plan living space without overwhelming the minimalist aesthetic.

We used white oak with a custom matte finish to create a sense of warmth, paired with hidden handles and integrated appliances for a seamless visual flow.
